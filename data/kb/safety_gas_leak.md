# What To Do If You Smell Gas

If you suspect a natural-gas leak inside or outside your property,
**leave the area immediately** and call NeoVolt's 24/7 gas emergency
line from a safe location. Treat any noticeable rotten-egg smell as
a leak until proven otherwise.

## Do

- Leave the building or area on foot.
- Once outside and at least 30 metres away, call **1-800-NEO-GAS**
  (1-800-636-4427).
- Warn neighbours if the smell is outdoors and may affect them.
- Wait outside until NeoVolt's responder confirms the area is safe.

## Do NOT

- Do **not** flip light switches, plug or unplug anything, or use any
  electrical device — sparks can ignite leaked gas.
- Do **not** light a match, lighter, candle, or stove.
- Do **not** start a vehicle inside a garage attached to the
  property.
- Do **not** call from inside the building — leave first, then call.

## After the call

- A NeoVolt safety crew is dispatched on a priority response, with a
  target on-site time of 60 minutes for inhabited residential
  properties.
- Do not re-enter until the crew has cleared the building.
- A follow-up inspection is scheduled within 5 business days even
  after the immediate hazard is resolved.

## Exceptions

- If the smell is outdoors and clearly traceable to a damaged street
  line (e.g. construction nearby), call 911 first, then NeoVolt.
- Carbon-monoxide alarms are a separate hazard with a different
  protocol; CO has no smell.
