# Residential Billing Cycle and Due Dates

NeoVolt issues residential bills monthly. A statement is generated on
the 1st of each month and is due 21 calendar days after the issue
date. Customers receive the statement by email if enrolled in
paperless billing, or by post within 5 business days otherwise.

## Statement schedule

- Statement issued: 1st of each month.
- Standard due date: 22nd of the same month (21 days after issue).
- Autopay charges: same day as the statement issue date for enrolled
  customers — no due-date mechanics apply.

## Reading your statement

- Your account number and meter ID appear in the top-right corner.
- The "Charges" section breaks down energy delivery, supply, and any
  applicable fees.
- "Payments received" credits any payment posted between the prior
  statement and the current one.

## Exceptions

- New connections: the first bill is prorated from the connection
  date and may cover up to 45 days. The 21-day window still applies.
- Move-out final bills: due 14 calendar days after issue, not 21.
- Payment plan participants: see `payment_plan_arrears` for the plan
  schedule, which supersedes the standard due date.
