# Standard Residential Tariff (Flat Rate)

The standard residential tariff is a flat rate of **$0.142 per kWh**
plus a **$11.50 monthly service charge**. This is the default plan
for any new residential connection unless the customer enrolls in a
different tariff.

## Components

- **Energy charge**: $0.142 per kWh consumed in the billing period.
- **Service charge**: $11.50 flat per month, prorated to the day for
  partial months on connection or disconnection.
- **Regulated pass-throughs**: line items for grid-modernization
  assessment ($1.20/month) and renewable-portfolio compliance
  ($0.004/kWh) appear separately on the statement.

## Effective dates

- Current rate effective 2026-01-01, set by State Energy Regulator
  Order 2025-114.
- Next scheduled rate review: 2026-12-15.

## Exceptions

- Customers on a time-of-use plan pay peak/off-peak rates instead —
  see `tariff_time_of_use`.
- Pre-2023 grandfathered plans have different rates and remain
  closed to new enrollment — see `tariff_legacy_grandfathered`.
- Income-qualified customers may be eligible for a $0.012/kWh
  discount under the State Affordability Rider; eligibility is
  verified through the State Energy Regulator.
