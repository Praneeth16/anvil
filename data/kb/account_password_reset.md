# Resetting Your Online Account Password

You can reset your NeoVolt portal password yourself from the login
page. The link is valid for 30 minutes and may only be used once.

## Steps

1. Go to portal.neovolt.example/login.
2. Click "Forgot password" beneath the password field.
3. Enter the email address registered to your account.
4. Open the password-reset email NeoVolt sends within 5 minutes and
   click the link inside.
5. Choose a new password (minimum 12 characters, including at least
   one number and one symbol).
6. Confirm and log in with the new password.

## Troubleshooting

- **No email received within 10 minutes**: check spam or quarantine,
  then re-request the reset. Multiple requests invalidate the older
  links.
- **Link expired**: request a new reset; old links are intentionally
  short-lived for security.
- **You no longer have access to the registered email**: call
  customer support — see `emergency_contact_24_7` for the 24/7
  number, or use 1-800-NEOVOLT during business hours.

## Exceptions

- Multi-factor authentication (MFA), once enabled, also requires a
  one-time code in addition to the new password. If you've lost
  access to your MFA device, customer support can verify identity
  by other means.
- Business accounts use a separate identity provider; this flow does
  not apply to them.
