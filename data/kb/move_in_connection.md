# Starting Service at a New Residential Address

NeoVolt can start residential service at a new address with at least
**3 business days' notice**. Same-day connections are not available
for residential customers; expedited 1-business-day connection is
available for a $45 fee.

## How to request

1. Log in to portal.neovolt.example or call 1-800-NEOVOLT.
2. Provide the new service address and the desired start date.
3. Confirm identity with the last four digits of your government ID
   or an existing NeoVolt account number.
4. Choose a tariff. New customers default to the standard
   residential tariff if none is selected.

## What happens next

- A connection order is dispatched. For most addresses, no on-site
  visit is required — the meter is energized remotely.
- If the address has not been served before, an installer visits
  during a 4-hour window on the connection date.
- A welcome email is sent the morning of the connection with the
  account number and first-bill estimate.

## Fees

- **Standard connection**: free with 3 business days' notice.
- **Expedited (1 business day)**: $45.
- **New-address build-out**: if the property has never had service,
  build-out costs are quoted separately based on distance to the
  nearest grid tie-in.

## Exceptions

- Disconnection at your previous address is a separate request —
  do not assume move-in starts a move-out automatically. See the
  service-disconnection request flow in the portal.
- Income-qualified customers may waive the expedited fee under the
  State Affordability Rider.
