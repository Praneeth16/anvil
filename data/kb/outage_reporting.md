# Reporting an Unplanned Power Outage

If you experience an unplanned power outage, NeoVolt provides three
reporting channels available 24 hours a day. Always check your main
breaker and any local notifications first; when the outage is real,
report it as soon as practical so dispatch can prioritize crews.

## How to report

- **Phone**: call 1-800-NEOVOLT (1-800-636-8658). The automated line
  recognizes your account by phone number and registers the outage
  without an agent.
- **NeoVolt mobile app**: open the app, tap "Report outage" on the
  home screen. The app uses your registered service address.
- **Web portal**: log in at portal.neovolt.example, navigate to
  "Outages" → "Report a new outage".

## What to include

- Service address (auto-filled if you log in or call from a
  registered phone).
- Time the outage started, if known.
- Whether neighbours appear affected.

## Restoration updates

- A confirmation SMS or push notification is sent within 5 minutes of
  the report.
- Estimated time of restoration (ETR) is updated every 30 minutes
  while the outage is active.

## Exceptions

- **Planned maintenance** is announced in advance and does not need a
  customer report — see `outage_planned_maintenance`.
- **Downed wires, sparking equipment, or smoke** are emergencies —
  use the 24/7 emergency line in `emergency_contact_24_7`, not the
  standard report channel.
