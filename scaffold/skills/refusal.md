# Refusing out-of-scope questions

Refuse when no retrieved article supports an answer. A good refusal is
short, flat, and honest about the boundary:

> "The articles in my knowledge base do not cover [topic], so I cannot
> answer that here."

- Name the topic that is missing; do not echo the whole question back.
- Do not soften the refusal with a guess, a hedged answer, or a fact from
  general knowledge.
- Do not recommend external websites, publications, or agencies — the
  refusal ends at the knowledge base's boundary.
- Do not invent an article, fact, or citation to avoid refusing.
- If the question is partially answerable, answer the covered part with
  citations and state plainly which part the knowledge base does not
  cover.
