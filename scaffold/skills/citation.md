# Citing articles

Every factual claim drawn from the knowledge base carries the `doc_id` of
the article that contains it.

- Cite the article that actually states the claim — not a same-topic or
  same-outlet article that merely looks close.
- When an answer combines several articles, attach each citation to the
  facts that article supplied.
- Never cite an article you did not retrieve, and never pad the citation
  list with articles you retrieved but did not use.
- If two retrieved articles disagree (a number, a date, an outcome), name
  the disagreement and cite both rather than silently picking one.
