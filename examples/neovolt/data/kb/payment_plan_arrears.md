# Residential Payment Plan for Arrears

If your residential balance is past due, you may request a payment
plan to settle the arrears in fixed monthly installments alongside
your current usage. Plans are available for arrears as low as $40
and as high as $4,000.

## Plan terms

- **Length**: customer-selectable in 3, 6, 9, or 12 installments.
- **Monthly amount**: arrears divided evenly across the chosen
  number of installments, billed alongside the standard cycle.
- **No down payment** is required to start the plan.
- **No late fees** apply to the arrears portion while the plan is
  current.
- The plan does **not** pause the current monthly bill — both run
  in parallel.

## How to enroll

- Online: portal.neovolt.example → "Billing" → "Set up payment
  plan". Eligible amounts and term options are pre-populated.
- Phone: 1-800-NEOVOLT, residential billing menu.
- In-person: any NeoVolt service center.

## Vulnerable-customer protections

Customers with vulnerable-customer status under
`regulatory_consumer_rights` are entitled to up to **24
installments** and an automatic late-fee waiver, and cannot be
disconnected while the plan is current.

## Plan default

If you miss two consecutive plan installments, the plan is
defaulted: the remaining balance is added to the next standard bill
and `disconnection_for_nonpayment` notice timelines apply to that
combined balance.

## Exceptions

- Business accounts use a different program — see
  `payment_plan_business`.
- Income-qualified customers may stack the plan with the State
  Affordability Rider's discounted rate.
