# Submitting a Self Meter Reading

If your meter could not be read by NeoVolt during the scheduled
field visit, you can submit a self-reading and avoid an estimated
bill. Self-readings are accepted up to **3 calendar days** after the
statement issue date for the current cycle.

## How to submit

- **App**: NeoVolt mobile app → "Meters" → "Submit reading". Take a
  clear photo of the dial; the app extracts the digits automatically.
- **Web portal**: portal.neovolt.example → "Meters" → "Submit
  reading". Type the digits in or upload a photo.
- **Phone**: call 1-800-NEOVOLT and follow the meter-reading menu.

## What to enter

- All digits shown on the main dial, left to right, ignoring
  decimals or trailing zeros after the decimal.
- The reading date — defaults to today if you do not specify.

## What happens next

- A self-reading replaces the estimated value used for the current
  statement.
- If the statement has already issued, a corrected statement is
  generated within 1 business day at no charge.

## Exceptions

- **Smart meters** report automatically every 15 minutes — no
  self-reading is needed unless the device has been offline for more
  than 7 days. See `smart_meter_required_for_tou` for smart-meter
  details.
- **Industrial AMI meters** at small-business or industrial sites
  use a different submission flow — see `meter_industrial_advanced`.
- A reading more than 25% lower than the prior reading is flagged
  for verification; expect a follow-up call within 2 business days.
