# Planned Maintenance Outages

NeoVolt schedules **planned outages** to perform substation upgrades,
line maintenance, and equipment swaps. These are announced in
advance and are **distinct** from unplanned outages caused by storms,
equipment failure, or accidents.

## How notice is given

- **At least 5 calendar days' notice** by email and SMS to all
  customers within the affected zone.
- A second reminder is sent **24 hours** before the start time.
- The portal's "Outage map" highlights upcoming planned outages.

## Standard windows

- Planned outages are scheduled during **off-peak hours**, typically
  09:00–14:00 on weekdays, to minimize customer impact.
- Total duration is usually **2–4 hours**; emergency-equipment swaps
  may extend up to 6 hours but are pre-announced.

## What customers should do

- Power down sensitive electronics 10 minutes before the scheduled
  start.
- For medical-equipment users, call the registration desk in
  `regulatory_consumer_rights` to confirm an exception or arrange a
  reschedule.
- No customer report is needed — see `outage_reporting` for the
  unplanned-outage flow, which does not apply here.

## Compensation

- **Planned outages are NOT eligible** for the outage compensation
  credits described in `outage_compensation`. Compensation applies
  only to **unplanned** outages exceeding 12 continuous hours.

## Cancellation and rescheduling

- NeoVolt may cancel a planned outage if weather conditions prevent
  safe work; affected customers are notified by SMS and email.
- A rescheduled outage is treated as a new planned outage with a
  fresh 5-day notice period.
