# Legacy Grandfathered Tariff Plans (Closed)

NeoVolt operated three legacy residential tariffs prior to the 2023
rate consolidation. These plans are **closed to new enrollment**.
Customers who were already on a legacy plan as of **2023-01-01** may
remain on it indefinitely or switch to a current plan (the switch
is one-way — they cannot return).

## Closed plans

- **Plan A — Flat 2018**: $0.085/kWh + $7.00 monthly service charge.
  Closed 2023-01-01.
- **Plan B — Two-period 2019**: peak $0.146 / off-peak $0.071,
  6-hour peak window 17:00–23:00. Closed 2023-01-01.
- **Plan C — Heat-pump rebate 2020**: $0.092/kWh with a $40 monthly
  bill credit on heat-pump heating loads. Closed 2023-01-01.

## How to identify if you are on a legacy plan

- The plan name appears under "Tariff" on your statement (e.g.
  "Plan A — Flat 2018").
- Legacy plans show a "Legacy" tag on the statement header.

## Restrictions while on a legacy plan

- **No** mid-cycle tariff changes. Customers on legacy plans can
  switch to a current plan at any time, but only at the start of a
  billing cycle.
- **Smart-meter installation is still available** — switching to a
  smart meter does not move you off your legacy plan unless you
  also request a tariff change.
- **No participation** in current promotional rates, demand-response
  programs, or income-qualified discounts. Income-qualified
  customers must switch to a current plan to access the State
  Affordability Rider.

## Important

The current standard residential tariff (`tariff_standard_residential`)
charges **$0.142/kWh + $11.50 monthly service charge** as of
2026-04-01. This is **not** the same as Plan A's historical $0.085;
do not assume a current customer pays the legacy rate based on
older communications.
