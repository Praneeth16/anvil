# Consumer Rights and Vulnerable-Customer Protections

Residential customers are protected by State Energy Regulator
Orders 2024-082 (disconnection) and 2025-044 (vulnerable customers).
This page summarizes the rights NeoVolt is bound to honour and the
process to escalate to the regulator if those rights are violated.

## Vulnerable-customer status

Eligibility — a customer who certifies one of:

- Receives state income support or disability benefits.
- Is over 70 years of age.
- Has medically-essential equipment registered (life support,
  dialysis, oxygen concentrator, etc.).
- Has primary care of a child under 12 months in the household.

Protections granted on registration:

- **Late fees waived** (see `late_fee_policy`).
- **Up to 24 installments** on payment plans (see
  `payment_plan_arrears`).
- **Cannot be disconnected** for non-payment as long as a payment
  plan is current.
- **Priority restoration** during outages.

## Disconnection rights

- 15 business days' written notice required (see
  `disconnection_for_nonpayment`).
- No disconnection on Fridays, weekends, public holidays, or the
  business day before a public holiday.
- Extreme-weather moratoria pause disconnections during declared
  heatwaves and cold snaps.

## Outage rights

- A confirmation that an outage report has been received within
  5 minutes.
- Estimated time of restoration updated every 30 minutes.
- Compensation for prolonged outages — see `outage_compensation`.

## Complaints process

1. Try NeoVolt customer support first: 1-800-NEOVOLT, business
   hours.
2. If unresolved within 14 calendar days, escalate to the State
   Energy Regulator at regulator.example/complaints.
3. The regulator will assign a case number within 5 business days
   and require NeoVolt's written response within 21 business days.
