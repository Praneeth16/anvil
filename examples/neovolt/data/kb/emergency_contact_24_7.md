# 24/7 Emergency Contact Lines

NeoVolt operates two separate 24/7 emergency lines. Use them only
for **safety emergencies and active-hazard situations**. Routine
outage reports use the standard channels in `outage_reporting`.

## Emergency lines

- **Gas emergency**: **1-800-NEO-GAS** (1-800-636-4427). Call from
  outside the affected area for any suspected gas leak — see
  `safety_gas_leak` for the do/do-not protocol.
- **Power emergency**: **1-800-NEO-PWR** (1-800-636-7977). Call for
  downed wires, sparking equipment, fallen poles, or any
  electrical situation that poses an immediate safety risk.

Both lines are answered by a live operator within 30 seconds.

## What counts as an emergency

- **Gas**: smell of gas, hissing from a meter or street line,
  visible damage to gas equipment.
- **Power**: downed power lines (even if not sparking), trees on
  lines, exposed conductors, or any condition where a bystander
  could be electrocuted.
- **Storm-related**: structural damage to NeoVolt equipment from
  wind, ice, or impact.

## What is NOT an emergency

- A standard outage with no visible hazard — use
  `outage_reporting`.
- A high bill or billing dispute — use customer support during
  business hours.
- A planned-maintenance notice question — see
  `outage_planned_maintenance`.

## Multi-language support

Both lines have menu prompts for English, Spanish, and French. If
your preferred language is not offered, the operator can transfer
to a translation service.
